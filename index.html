DJDL Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

DJDL is a library app for DJs. This policy covers the DJDL app for iPhone.

What DJDL collects

Nothing. DJDL has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. It doesn't collect, store or share any personal information, and nothing about you or your music is sent to the developer.

Your songs and settings

Your library, its songs and your settings stay on your iPhone, in DJDL's folder in the Files app. If you use iCloud Drive or iPhone backups, Apple stores those copies under Apple's own privacy policy.

Internet use

  • When you tap a song in Find Songs, Safari opens that song's page on Beatport. Whatever happens there is covered by Beatport's privacy policy. DJDL doesn't send Beatport anything else.
  • When you paste a public playlist link, DJDL reads that page to get the song names. Only the link you pasted is requested.
  • When you add a link to a song file (for example your own website, Dropbox or Google Drive), DJDL downloads that file to your iPhone.

Children

DJDL doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
